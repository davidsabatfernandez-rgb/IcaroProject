## Access limitations

Unsplash, Pexels, and Wikimedia domains return proxy HTTP 403 in this environment, including after a normal network escalation. Source pages were therefore not fetched directly; the images and attribution evidence were downloaded from the pinned GitHub mirrors above. Do not claim a fresh verification of source page contents. The original source URLs and standard license references are preserved for review.

The downloaded black-and-white assets from sam-timmins/T4Tri-triathlon-club have only a general Unsplash reference under tools; they are not selected here because per-file license/source mappings could not be established.
